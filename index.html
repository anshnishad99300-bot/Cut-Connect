About Cut Connect

Creative work, connected.

Cut Connect is an India-focused creative services marketplace. Clients discover video editors, designers, writers and other creative professionals, or post a project and receive proposals. Creators showcase their skills and apply for work.

Cut Connect connects clients and creators. It does not process payments: agree on scope, price and payment directly with the other person.

Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who can use Cut Connect

Cut Connect is only for people who are 18 years of age or older. By creating an account you confirm that your date of birth is correct and that you are at least 18.

2. Your account

Keep your login details safe and give correct information. You are responsible for everything done through your account.

3. Clients and creators

Clients can post projects and hire creators. Creators can showcase their work and apply for projects. Cut Connect helps people find each other; the agreement for any work is between the client and the creator.

4. Orders and payments

Scope, delivery time and price should be agreed clearly before work starts. Cut Connect does not process payments. Agree on the price and payment method directly with the other person.

5. Your content

You own the work and content you upload. You confirm you have the right to share it and that it does not copy someone else's work.

6. What is not allowed

Fake profiles, fraud, harassment, illegal or adult content, spam, and misuse of other people's work are not allowed. Accounts that break these rules may be removed.

7. Privacy

We ask for details such as name, date of birth and language only to set up your account and check the 18+ rule. Your date of birth, gender and language are stored privately and are not shown to other users.

8. Changes and contact

We may update these terms from time to time. For questions, use Help & Feedback.
